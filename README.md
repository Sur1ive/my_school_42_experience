## Piscine Projects
The piscine is a one-month sprint that tests your burst capacity and time management. There is never enough time—you basically can't finish every project. You can adopt some strategies; for example, the last exercise of some projects is an algorithm problem that is a big step harder, so the safe move is to skip it instead of chasing a perfect score, then come back to max it out at the end if you have energy to spare.

Final admission isn't based on exam scores alone, so don't fixate on them. While pushing your project progress as hard as you can, take part in events and team projects and socialize a lot—this maximizes your admission rate.
